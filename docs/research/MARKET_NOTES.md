# Market Notes

Last updated: 2026-05-03

## Seed observations

- The market splits into at least four jobs: system-wide dictation, file transcription, meeting transcription, and voice-note/searchable-memory workflows.
- Competitors increasingly market AI cleanup, contextual rewriting, multilingual support, and privacy/trust.
- Local processing is valuable but not enough; users also need fast setup, clear states, reliable insertion, and high-quality rewritten output.
- Meeting transcription products have different activation loops from push-to-talk dictation products.
